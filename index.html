Strategic priorities — why performance is capped

In the order you raised them
1

Custom-intent audiences aren't being built

Most PPC and Paid Social budget is running on broad or interest-based targeting rather than custom-intent audiences built from your own funnel (visitors of specific rate/product pages, users who searched competitor+category terms, lookalikes of funded accounts). This is very likely why CPA is high and Accounts→Funded is low in exactly the countries above (KSA, Morocco, Tunisia, Switzerland) — the traffic converts to a lead but isn't pre-qualified for funding.

Fix: build a custom-intent keyword and audience seed list per country from your own search-term reports, CRM funded-account list, and competitor terms — Claude can generate and maintain this list on a weekly cadence directly from the PBI search-term exports.

2

Competitor-client targeting is missing entirely

None of the current spend appears to target competitors' clients or brand-search traffic — standard practice for every other forex/CFD broker. This is the highest-leverage gap: competitor-brand search campaigns and lookalikes of competitor social followers typically convert at a materially lower CPA than cold prospecting, because the audience already understands the product category.

Fix: launch competitor conquesting campaigns (Google Ads competitor-brand keywords, Meta lookalikes seeded from competitor page engagement) in the top 5 markets by spend (UAE, KSA, Germany, Canada, Australia) as a controlled test before wider rollout.

3

No CRM/automation layer connecting ad platforms to sales

Without a CRM and marketing-automation layer (e.g. HubSpot) sitting between the ad platforms and the sales desks, lead scoring, remarketing lists, and audience sync all have to be rebuilt by hand per channel — which is almost certainly contributing to the funding-stage drop-off seen above.
