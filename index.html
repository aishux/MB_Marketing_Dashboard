Reporting cadence

What gets checked, and how often
Daily

Spend pacing and anomalies

  • Yesterday's spend, leads and CPL against the daily plan pace for every country and channel
  • Flag any country or channel with CPC or CPL more than 50% above its 7 day average
  • Flag any campaign that spent but produced zero clicks, a tracking or creative issue
Weekly

Funnel conversion and reallocation

  • Leads to accounts and accounts to funded rates by country and channel, week over week
  • Recommended dollar shifts between countries and channels, as above
  • Creative and audience fatigue check for Paid Social
Monthly

ROI, CPFA and budget plan review

  • Full month ROI and CPFA against the marketing budget plan, by region
  • Which countries and channels earn a bigger share of next month's budget
  • Progress against the three strategic priorities

Why performance is capped

Three priorities, in the order raised
1Targeting

Custom intent audiences are not being built

Most budget runs on broad or interest based targeting instead of audiences built from your own funnel data.

  • No audiences built from rate or product page visitors, competitor plus category search terms, or lookalikes of funded accounts
  • This lines up with the pattern in the data: KSA, Morocco, Tunisia and Switzerland generate leads cheaply but convert almost none of them to funded accounts
Fix: build a custom intent keyword and audience seed list per country from search term reports, the CRM's funded account list, and competitor terms. Claude can generate and maintain this weekly from the PBI search term exports.
2Competitive gap

Competitor client targeting is missing entirely

No current spend targets competitors' clients or brand search traffic, standard practice across the industry.

  • Competitor brand search and lookalikes of competitor social followers typically cost less per acquisition than cold prospecting, because the audience already understands the product
  • This is the single highest leverage gap in the current setup
Fix: launch competitor conquesting campaigns, Google Ads on competitor brand keywords and Meta lookalikes seeded from competitor page engagement, as a controlled test in the top five markets by spend (UAE, KSA, Germany, Canada, Australia) before wider rollout.
3Infrastructure

No CRM or automation layer connecting ad platforms to sales

Without a CRM such as HubSpot between the ad platforms and the sales desks, lead scoring and audience sync have to be rebuilt by hand for every channel, which is likely contributing to the funding stage drop off above.
