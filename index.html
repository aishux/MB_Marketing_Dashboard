Where to move the budget next month

Simple rules, applied to every country and channel: scale what funds accounts, cut what does not, hold the rest.
Country Channel Spend (Aug) ROI Funded Action Suggested change Why

The rules behind the table: Increase means ROI was 15% or better with at least one funded account, so we suggest adding about 25% to its budget. Decrease means at least $2,000 was spent with zero funded accounts, so we suggest cutting about 35%. Hold is everything in between, plus countries where spend was too small to judge. Before cutting the zero funded countries, see priority 3 on the Strategic Priorities tab.

Three strategic moves for September

Only confirmed, data backed points. Everything below comes from the August numbers on the other tabs.
1
Competitive gap

We are not targeting competitor clients at all

None of the current spend bids on competitor brand searches or builds audiences from competitor followers. This is standard practice across the broker industry, and those audiences usually cost less to win because they already understand the product. Right now, all of these brands are left completely uncontested:

Start with a controlled test in the five biggest markets by August spend:

The fix: launch competitor conquesting as a test. Google Ads on competitor brand keywords, plus Meta lookalike audiences built from competitor page engagement. Run it in the five markets above first, measure cost per funded account against current campaigns, then roll out wider if it wins.
